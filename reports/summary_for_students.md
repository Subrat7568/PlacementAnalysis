# The Playbook: What Actually Gets You Placed

**Headline finding:** Your branch barely matters — what you *do* with your time
in college is what moves the needle.

Here's why, based on three things in the data:

**1. Branch is basically a coin flip.** Whether you're in IT, Mechanical, Civil,
or anything else, the average package only moves by about ₹1.5 lakh across all
six branches (₹63.8L to ₹65.4L). So if you're stressing about "is my branch good
enough," you can stop — it isn't the deciding factor here.

**2. Big companies hire the most, but they're not the only door.** MNCs took in
the largest number of students by far, so yes, they're a solid target. But
"Top Tech" companies — even though they hired far fewer people — paid noticeably
more on average. Don't put all your eggs in one basket chasing only the
biggest, most obvious names. Startups and mid-size companies are still real,
valid paths.

**3. Where you study creates a real gap, and it's the biggest one in the data.**
Students from Tier 1 colleges got placed about 94% of the time. Tier 3
students, about 76%. That's an 18-point gap — bigger than any branch or
company-type difference. This is the one thing that actually separates
students clearly.

## Recommendation

If you're at a Tier 1 college, you already have some wind at your back — don't
get complacent. If you're at a Tier 2 or Tier 3 college, that 18-point gap is
exactly what you need to close yourself: build real projects, land internships,
and keep your academics clean (no backlogs). None of that depends on your
branch or your college's name — it depends on you showing up with a stronger
profile than the average student around you. **That's the one lever you fully
control, so pull it.**
